# KWIN City backlink outreach playbook

## Objective

Earn a small number of editorial, relevant links to `https://kwin-city.com/` and its evidence resources. The priority is trust and referral value, not link volume.

Primary destination for first outreach touchpoints:

- `https://kwin-city.com/press` (citation-ready media kit)

## Positioning to use consistently

`kwin-city.com` is an **independent, source-linked research portal** about the proposed KWIN City development. It is not the official KIADB project website. It distinguishes confirmed material, proposals, and regional context, and links readers to the underlying sources.

Use this exact description when a short attribution is needed:

> Independent, source-linked research portal for KWIN City and the North Bengaluru development context.

## Link destination matrix

- `https://kwin-city.com/press` for first-touch editorial requests and attribution guidance.
- `https://kwin-city.com/sources` for claim verification and source-ledger coverage.
- `https://kwin-city.com/evidence` for methodology and dataset-limit discussions.
- `https://kwin-city.com/data-insights` for chart embeds, data visual references, and regional trend stories.
- `https://kwin-city.com/downloads` for document-library citations.

## Qualification rules

- Prioritise government, institutional, established editorial, academic, and nonprofit sites.
- Seek a contextual, editorially chosen link on a relevant page—not a footer, sitewide link, or paid placement.
- Do not buy links, use link exchanges, submit to bulk directories, or ask partners to use deceptive anchor text.
- Never describe this portal as official unless KIADB has explicitly approved that language.
- Link to the most useful canonical page; do not route all links to the home page.

## Outreach sequence

1. Start with KIADB. Ask only for a clearly labelled, optional resource link that accurately distinguishes this portal from the official project site.
2. Make one substantive contribution before approaching industry associations: a short, source-linked briefing, an event note, or a reusable regional-data visual.
3. Pitch reporters only when there is a real news hook, newly verified source, or public update. Do not pitch a generic “please link to us” request.
4. Record each outreach in the tracker below. Follow up once after 7–10 business days, then close the request.
5. Review referring domains monthly in Bing Webmaster Tools and Google Search Console; check that new links resolve to canonical HTTPS URLs.

## High-conversion editorial assets (build once, reuse often)

- A one-page “what changed this month” evidence brief that links to `/updates` and `/sources`.
- A chart-first mini analysis from `/data-insights` tied to one concrete local policy question.
- A claim clarification note (200–300 words) that resolves one common misconception using `/sources`.
- A reporter shortcut card that maps “angle -> best destination page” from the matrix above.

## Source notes (checked 17 August 2026)

- KIADB publishes a dedicated KWIN City page, making it the highest-value topical authority for a clearly labelled resource reference.
- Invest Karnataka published a KWIN City launch release describing the project’s public context and media contact pathway.
- GBITCIA describes its role in Bengaluru industry, policy, infrastructure, and strategic partnerships, which makes a contribution-led approach more appropriate than a cold link exchange.
- Commercial property pages that mention KWIN City were deliberately excluded from the priority list because they are not a reliable substitute for editorial or institutional authority.
