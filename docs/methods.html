Methods

Confidence should be visible.

We distinguish an institutional record, a project proposal, and a regional inference because each answers a different question.

Verified record

Supported by a primary institutional document or responsible authority. Cite the originating record.

Project proposal

Reported in a brief, statement, or announcement. It may be meaningful, but requires further confirmation.

Regional context

Evidence about North Bengaluru that frames conditions but cannot prove a KWIN-specific outcome.

Source hierarchy

We prefer statutory records and responsible institutions; then primary datasets; then transparent public-interest repositories; then clearly-labelled reporting; and finally derived analysis with its assumptions stated.

What we will not do

We do not convert regional momentum into a delivery guarantee, publish personal land or contact data, or present scenario modelling as an investment recommendation. Read the live claim ledger before relying on a statement.
