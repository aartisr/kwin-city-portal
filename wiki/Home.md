## Documentation conventions

This Wiki uses plain, source-conscious language. “Verified” means a primary record supports a statement; “proposed” means a plan or public statement requires further confirmation; “contextual” means evidence explains the region without proving a KWIN-specific result. Read the [Evidence Standard](Evidence-Standard.md) before quoting project figures.
