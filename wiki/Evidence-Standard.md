# Evidence Standard

Each claim should be labelled by what it can support:

| Label | Meaning |
| --- | --- |
| Verified record | A primary institutional or statutory source supports the claim. |
| Project proposal | A brief, public statement, or reported plan describes the claim; further confirmation may be needed. |
| Regional context | A source describes North Bengaluru or a wider system, not a KWIN-specific result. |

Sources should be cited with a stable URL, publisher, publication date, relevant page/section, and a plain-language note on limitations.

## Minimum evidence record

For each material claim, record:

- Claim text and the page where it appears.
- Source title, publisher, date, and durable URL.
- Exact relevant page, section, table, or passage.
- Classification and confidence rationale.
- What the source cannot prove.
- Review date and the person or process that performed the review.

## Language rules

Use “states,” “reports,” “proposes,” or “indicates” when describing a source. Reserve “confirms” for evidence that directly establishes the claim. Avoid “will,” “guarantees,” “approved,” and similar definitive language unless an authoritative current record supports it.
