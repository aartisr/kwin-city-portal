# Citation and Contribution

Suggested citation:

> KWIN City Research Portal. Independent evidence-first research on KWIN City and North Bengaluru. https://kwin-city.com/

When contributing a correction, include the exact claim, original source URL, relevant page/date, and a concise explanation. Do not post personal information, confidential files, or land-title documents in public issues.

Open a [GitHub issue](https://github.com/aartisr/kwin-city-portal/issues) or contact the [research desk](https://kwin-city.com/contact).

## Editorial contribution protocol

1. Search existing issues first.
2. Provide the original source rather than a screenshot, paraphrase, or search result.
3. Explain the source’s relevance and any known limitation.
4. Keep submissions factual and avoid personal, financial, or land-record details.
5. Expect a review for accuracy, scope, and wording before publication.

Contributions improve the portal when they add verifiable evidence, clarify a limitation, or correct a meaningful error.
