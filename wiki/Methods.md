# Methods

The research workflow is straightforward:

1. Frame the question precisely.
2. Find the closest responsible authority or original document.
3. Record what the source supports and what it cannot support.
4. Separate fact, proposal, interpretation, and scenario.
5. Publish a correction when stronger evidence emerges.

## Review checklist

Before publishing or materially updating a claim, check that the destination page has a clear title, source link, classification, date context, limitation statement, and a meaningful next action for a reader. For numerical claims, verify units, period, geography, methodology, and whether the number is historical, projected, or scenario-based.

## Reproducibility

When a result is derived rather than quoted, preserve the input source, transformation logic, assumptions, and date accessed. A reader should be able to understand how the result was produced even if a third-party source later changes.
