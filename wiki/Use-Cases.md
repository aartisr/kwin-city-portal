# Use Cases

## Journalists

Start with the [Evidence Vault](https://kwin-city.com/evidence). Attribute original institutions directly and label unresolved claims.

## Researchers

Use the [Data Insights](https://kwin-city.com/insights) and source hierarchy to distinguish regional evidence from project-specific records.

## Landowners and residents

Use the [Risk Check](https://kwin-city.com/risks) for preliminary research only. Verify survey, title, and statutory questions with responsible authorities and qualified professionals.

## Investors and institutions

Use the [Regulatory Navigator](https://kwin-city.com/regulatory) and [Opportunity Exchange](https://kwin-city.com/opportunities) as structured starting points, not as investment or allotment guarantees.

## Public agencies and civic groups

Use the portal to identify questions, compare public records, and improve public explanation. Do not treat portal content as a statutory notice or an official substitute for the authority responsible for a process.

## Appropriate use boundary

The portal can help formulate a question; it cannot settle title, regulatory status, investment suitability, engineering feasibility, or legal entitlement.
