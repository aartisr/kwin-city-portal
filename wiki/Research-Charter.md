# Research Charter

The portal exists to make KWIN City and North Bengaluru research more inspectable, not more promotional.

## Commitments

1. Link meaningful claims to the strongest available source.
2. Keep project proposals distinct from verified records.
3. State when regional evidence cannot establish a project-specific outcome.
4. Welcome corrections supported by stronger primary evidence.
5. Protect privacy: do not publish personal, land-title, or confidential material.

## Editorial independence

The portal is not an official project authority. Editorial work must never imply governmental endorsement, guaranteed delivery, land availability, investment return, or regulatory approval where the underlying evidence does not establish it.

## Corrections standard

Corrections should be specific, attributable, and durable. When stronger primary evidence conflicts with a portal interpretation, the primary source takes precedence and the portal should be amended with a clear record of the change.

Read the live [Evidence Vault](https://kwin-city.com/evidence) for claim-level context.
